Privacy policy

You can use this app without creating an account or entering your name or email address.

Your grade setting, questions, selected answers, correct answers, explanations, scores, active solving time, round dates and time zones, and recent question history are stored in the app's on-device storage. They support history review and help avoid repeated questions. The app has no feature that sends these solving records to a developer server.

The app may read the App Store country provided by Apple to choose its language. If unavailable, it uses the device region and language settings. It does not request GPS access or a separate account. This information is not used for advertising or tracking.

The current app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and no in-app purchases. Operating-system backups, restoration and Apple diagnostic sharing may depend on your settings and Apple's policies.

Deleting the app can remove its local app data. Offloading may keep that data. Existing backups and their restoration or deletion must be managed separately in your operating-system settings.

If you email support, the recipient receives the email address and message you choose to send. Please do not include unnecessary personal information such as your name, school or sensitive details. You can use the contact below to request deletion of your support correspondence.

This page contains no analytics tools or contact form. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Website visits are separate from the app’s local solving records.
