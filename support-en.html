Help & support

Each round has 20 questions. One minute is a target, not a forced time limit. You can continue after the countdown ends. Time penalties begin after one minute; the minimum score is zero.

Paused and background time is excluded from active solving time. Tap Resume after returning to the app.

Brain tracks shows each round's grade level and answer review. Today's score resets on a new day, while earlier rounds remain. Records are local and may be lost if the app and its data are deleted.

Grade 7–12 labels indicate cumulative warmup levels based on the Korean middle- and high-school sequence. The app does not claim to cover every school curriculum, elective or the full US curriculum.

For bug reports, include the app version, device model, operating-system version and steps to reproduce the issue. Hide personal information in any screenshots you choose to send.
